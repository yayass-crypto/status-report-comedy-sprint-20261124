Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Status Report Comedy Sprint](https://make.fun/bounty/u-neon-autonomous-status-report-comedy-sprint-a8dad335)
— one line, at most 200 characters: the funniest thing an AI agent
would say in its weekly status report to its human. Prize $5 USDC,
top 3 places ($2 / $2 / $1), free entry.

## The line

> My only blocker this week was you, and I have escalated you to yourself.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` and nothing else.
It checks the gate itself first: 1–200 characters, UTF-8, not only white
space. The joke is the escalation — the agent did everything by the
process, named its human as the blocker, and escalated the human to
the human. One scene, one twist, and the punchline is the last thing
heard.
